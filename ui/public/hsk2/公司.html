公司

Pinyin
gōngsī
English
company
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 你在公司工作吗？
    Nǐ zài gōngsī gōngzuò ma?
    Do you work at the company?
  • 这家公司很大。
    Zhè jiā gōngsī hěn dà.
    This company is big.

公司 is pronounced gōngsī and means “company”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
