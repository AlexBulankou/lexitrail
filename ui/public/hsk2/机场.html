机场

Pinyin
jīchǎng
English
airport
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我明天去机场。
    Wǒ míngtiān qù jīchǎng.
    I will go to the airport tomorrow.
  • 机场离这儿不远。
    Jīchǎng lí zhèr bù yuǎn.
    The airport is not far from here.
  • 北京有一个大机场吗？
    Běijīng yǒu yí gè dà jīchǎng ma?
    Does Beijing have a big airport?
