非常

Pinyin
fēicháng
English
very
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我非常喜欢这本书。
    Wǒ fēicháng xǐhuān zhè běn shū.
    I like this book very much.
  • 大家都非常高兴。
    Dàjiā dōu fēicháng gāoxìng.
    Everyone is extremely happy.
  • 我非常喜欢苹果。
    Wǒ fēicháng xǐhuan píngguǒ.
    I really like apples.
  • 他非常大。
    Tā fēicháng dà.
    He is extremely big.

非常 is pronounced fēicháng and means “very”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
