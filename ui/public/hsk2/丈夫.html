丈夫

Pinyin
zhàngfu
English
husband
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我丈夫在北京工作。
    Wǒ zhàngfu zài Běijīng gōngzuò.
    My husband works in Beijing.
  • 丈夫喜欢喝茶。
    Zhàngfu xǐhuān hē chá.
    My husband likes drinking tea.
  • 我丈夫在家吗？
    Wǒ zhàngfu zài jiā ma?
    Is my husband at home?
  • 她的丈夫是医生。
    Tā de zhàngfu shì yīshēng.
    Her husband is a doctor.

丈夫 is pronounced zhàngfu and means “husband”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
