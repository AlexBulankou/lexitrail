也

Pinyin
yě
English
also
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我也想去北京。
    Wǒ yě xiǎng qù Běijīng.
    I also want to go to Beijing.
  • 它也在这儿吃饭。
    Tā yě zài zhèr chīfàn.
    It is also eating here.
  • 我也有一本书。
    Wǒ yě yǒu yì běn shū.
    I also have a book.
  • 她也在学汉语。
    Tā yě zài xué Hànyǔ.
    She is also learning Chinese.

也 is pronounced yě and means “also”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
