小时

Pinyin
xiǎoshí
English
hour
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我看了一小时书。
    Wǒ kàn le yī xiǎoshí shū.
    I read for one hour.
  • 它睡了两小时。
    Tā shuì le liǎng xiǎoshí.
    It slept for two hours.
  • 我等了一个小时。
    Wǒ děng le yí gè xiǎoshí.
    I waited for one hour.
  • 你只有两小时吗？
    Nǐ zhǐ yǒu liǎng xiǎoshí ma?
    Do you only have two hours?

小时 is pronounced xiǎoshí and means “hour”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
