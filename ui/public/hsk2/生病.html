生病

Pinyin
shēngbìng
English
get ill, get sick
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 妹妹生病了。
    Mèimei shēngbìng le.
    Younger sister is sick.
  • 你为什么生病了？
    Nǐ wèishénme shēngbìng le?
    Why did you get sick?

生病 is pronounced shēngbìng and means “get ill, get sick”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
