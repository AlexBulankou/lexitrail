好吃

Pinyin
hǎochī
English
delicious
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 这个菜很好吃。
    Zhège cài hěn hǎochī.
    This dish is delicious.
  • 你觉得苹果好吃吗？
    Nǐ juédé píngguǒ hǎochī ma?
    Do you think apples are tasty?

好吃 is pronounced hǎochī and means “delicious”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
