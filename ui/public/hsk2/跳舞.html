跳舞

Pinyin
tiàowǔ
English
dance
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 妹妹在房间跳舞。
    Mèimei zài fángjiān tiàowǔ.
    Younger sister is dancing in the room.
  • 你会跳舞吗？
    Nǐ huì tiàowǔ ma?
    Can you dance?

跳舞 is pronounced tiàowǔ and means “dance”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
