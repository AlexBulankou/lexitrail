从

Pinyin
cóng
English
from
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我从北京回来了。
    Wǒ cóng Běijīng huílái le.
    I came back from Beijing.
  • 它从那儿跑过来。
    Tā cóng nàr pǎo guòlái.
    It ran over from there.
  • 我从家去学校。
    Wǒ cóng jiā qù xuéxiào.
    I go to school from home.
  • 他从北京来了吗？
    Tā cóng Běijīng lái le ma?
    Did he come from Beijing?

从 is pronounced cóng and means “from”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
