千

Pinyin
qiān
English
thousand
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 那儿有一千个人。
    Nàr yǒu yī qiān gèrén.
    There are one thousand people there.
  • 我想买一千本书。
    Wǒ xiǎng mǎi yī qiān běn shū.
    I want to buy one thousand books.
  • 他有一千块钱吗？
    Tā yǒu yì qiān kuài qián ma?
    Does he have one thousand yuan?
  • 我想买一千个苹果。
    Wǒ xiǎng mǎi yì qiān gè píngguǒ.
    I want to buy one thousand apples.

千 is pronounced qiān and means “thousand”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
