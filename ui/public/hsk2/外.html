外

Pinyin
wài
English
outside
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 它在外边吗？
    Tā zài wàibian ma?
    Is it outside?
  • 我在门外等您。
    Wǒ zài mén wài děng nín.
    I am waiting for you outside the door.
  • 她在外面吗？
    Tā zài wàimiàn ma?
    Is she outside?
  • 我不想去外面。
    Wǒ bù xiǎng qù wàimiàn.
    I don't want to go outside.

外 is pronounced wài and means “outside”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
