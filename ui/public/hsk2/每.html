每

Pinyin
měi
English
every, each
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 每个人都有名字。
    Měi gèrén dōu yǒu míngzi.
    Each person has a name.
  • 每年我和大家去北京。
    Měi nián wǒ hé dàjiā qù Běijīng.
    Every year I go to Beijing with everyone.
  • 每个人都来了。
    Měi gè rén dōu lái le.
    Everyone has come.
  • 我每天都学习汉语。
    Wǒ měi tiān dōu xuéxí Hànyǔ.
    I study Chinese every day.

每 is pronounced měi and means “every, each”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test. Recognising a word on a page and recalling it when you need it are different skills, and only the second survives a conversation — which is why LexiTrail shows you 每 again just before you would have forgotten it, rather than on a fixed schedule.
