身体

Pinyin
shēntǐ
English
body
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我身体很好。
    Wǒ shēntǐ hěn hǎo.
    My body is healthy.
  • 它的身体很小。
    Tā de shēntǐ hěn xiǎo.
    Its body is small.
  • 你身体好吗？
    Nǐ shēntǐ hǎo ma?
    Is your body well?
  • 我身体真好。
    Wǒ shēntǐ zhēn hǎo.
    I am really healthy.

身体 is pronounced shēntǐ and means “body”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
