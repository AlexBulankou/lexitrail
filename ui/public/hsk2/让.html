让

Pinyin
ràng
English
let
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 请让他进来。
    Qǐng ràng tā jìnlái.
    Please let him come in.
  • 妈妈让我吃米饭。
    Māma ràng wǒ chī mǐfàn.
    Mom asks me to eat rice.

让 is pronounced ràng and means “let”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
