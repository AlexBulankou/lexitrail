出

Pinyin
chū
English
come
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 请你出去。
    Qǐng nǐ chūqù.
    Please go out.
  • 他从教室出来吗？
    Tā cóng jiàoshì chūlái ma?
    Is he coming out of the classroom?

出 is pronounced chū and means “come”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
