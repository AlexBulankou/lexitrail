便宜

Pinyin
piányi
English
cheap
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 这个东西很便宜。
    Zhège dōngxi hěn piányi.
    This thing is very cheap.
  • 那件衣服不便宜。
    Nà jiàn yīfu bù piányi.
    That clothing is not cheap.

便宜 is pronounced piányi and means “cheap”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
