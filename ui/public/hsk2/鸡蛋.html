鸡蛋

Pinyin
jīdàn
English
egg
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 您吃鸡蛋吗？
    Nín chī jīdàn ma?
    Do you eat eggs?
  • 我买了十个鸡蛋。
    Wǒ mǎi le shí gè jīdàn.
    I bought ten eggs.
  • 我每天吃鸡蛋。
    Wǒ měitiān chī jīdàn.
    I eat eggs every day.
  • 你想要几个鸡蛋？
    Nǐ xiǎng yào jǐ gè jīdàn?
    How many eggs do you want?

鸡蛋 is pronounced jīdàn and means “egg”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
