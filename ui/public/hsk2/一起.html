一起

Pinyin
yìqǐ
English
together
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我和您一起去商店。
    Wǒ hé nín yīqǐ qù shāngdiàn.
    I go to the store together with you.
  • 它想和大家一起看电视。
    Tā xiǎng hé dàjiā yīqǐ kàn diànshì.
    It wants to watch TV together with everyone.
  • 我们一起去吃饭吧。
    Wǒmen yìqǐ qù chī fàn ba.
    Let's go eat together.
  • 你想和我一起看电影吗？
    Nǐ xiǎng hé wǒ yìqǐ kàn diànyǐng ma?
    Do you want to watch a movie together with me?

一起 is pronounced yìqǐ and means “together”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
