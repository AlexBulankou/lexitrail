送

Pinyin
sòng
English
present
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我想送你一个苹果。
    Wǒ xiǎng sòng nǐ yí gè píngguǒ.
    I want to give you an apple.
  • 他送了我一件衣服。
    Tā sòng le wǒ yí jiàn yīfu.
    He gave me a piece of clothing.

送 is pronounced sòng and means “present”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
