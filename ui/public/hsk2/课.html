Example sentences

  • 我们今天有汉语课。
    Wǒmen jīntiān yǒu Hànyǔ kè.
    We have a Chinese lesson today.
  • 我喜欢这儿的课。
    Wǒ xǐhuan zhèr de kè.
    I like the lessons here.

课 is pronounced kè and means “lesson”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
