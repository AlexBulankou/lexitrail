房间

Pinyin
fángjiān
English
room
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我在房间里看书。
    Wǒ zài fángjiān lǐ kàn shū.
    I am reading in the room.
  • 它在我的房间睡觉。
    Tā zài wǒ de fángjiān shuìjiào.
    It is sleeping in my room.
  • 我住在那个房间。
    Wǒ zhù zài nà gè fángjiān.
    I live in that room.
  • 这个房间很小。
    Zhè gè fángjiān hěn xiǎo.
    This room is very small.

房间 is pronounced fángjiān and means “room”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
