考试

Pinyin
kǎoshì
English
exam
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 明天有汉语考试。
    Míngtiān yǒu Hànyǔ kǎoshì.
    There is a Chinese exam tomorrow.
  • 他不想去考试。
    Tā bù xiǎng qù kǎoshì.
    He does not want to go take the exam.

考试 is pronounced kǎoshì and means “exam”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
