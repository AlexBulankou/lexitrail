姐姐

Pinyin
jiějie
English
older sister
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我姐姐在医院工作。
    Wǒ jiějie zài yīyuàn gōngzuò.
    My older sister works in a hospital.
  • 姐姐非常喜欢狗。
    Jiějie fēicháng xǐhuān gǒu.
    My older sister really likes dogs.
  • 姐姐在商店工作。
    Jiějie zài shāngdiàn gōngzuò.
    My older sister works in a shop.
  • 我姐姐很漂亮。
    Wǒ jiějie hěn piàoliang.
    My older sister is very beautiful.

姐姐 is pronounced jiějie and means “older sister”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
