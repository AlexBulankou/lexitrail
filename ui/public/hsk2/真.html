真

Pinyin
zhēn
English
really
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 这本书真好。
    Zhè běn shū zhēn hǎo.
    This book is really good.
  • 您真喜欢它吗？
    Nín zhēn xǐhuān tā ma?
    Do you really like it?
  • 这个苹果真大。
    Zhè gè píngguǒ zhēn dà.
    This apple is really big.
  • 你真好。
    Nǐ zhēn hǎo.
    You are really nice.

真 is pronounced zhēn and means “really”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
