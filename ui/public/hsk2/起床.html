起床

Pinyin
qǐchuáng
English
get up
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我早上七点起床。
    Wǒ zǎoshang qī diǎn qǐchuáng.
    I get up at seven in the morning.
  • 你什么时候起床？
    Nǐ shénme shíhou qǐchuáng?
    When do you get up?
