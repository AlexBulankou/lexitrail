正在

Pinyin
zhèngzài
English
be being
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 它正在吃米饭。
    Tā zhèngzài chī mǐfàn.
    It is eating rice.
  • 我正在看那本书。
    Wǒ zhèngzài kàn nà běn shū.
    I am reading that book.
  • 我正在看书。
    Wǒ zhèngzài kàn shū.
    I am reading a book right now.
  • 她正在吃饭。
    Tā zhèngzài chī fàn.
    She is eating right now.

正在 is pronounced zhèngzài and means “be being”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
