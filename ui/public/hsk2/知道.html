知道

Pinyin
zhīdao
English
know
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我知道你的名字。
    Wǒ zhīdao nǐ de míngzi.
    I know your name.
  • 他不知道这件事情。
    Tā bù zhīdao zhè jiàn shìqing.
    He does not know about this matter.

知道 is pronounced zhīdao and means “know”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
