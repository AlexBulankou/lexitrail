票

Pinyin
piào
English
ticket
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我有两票。
    Wǒ yǒu liǎng piào.
    I have two tickets.
  • 这票是我的。
    Zhè piào shì wǒ de.
    This ticket is mine.

票 is pronounced piào and means “ticket”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
