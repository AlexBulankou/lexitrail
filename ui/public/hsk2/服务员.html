服务员

Pinyin
fúwùyuán
English
waiter
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 那个服务员很好。
    Nàgè fúwùyuán hěn hǎo.
    That waiter is very nice.
  • 服务员在饭店里工作。
    Fúwùyuán zài fàndiàn lǐ gōngzuò.
    The waiter works in the restaurant.
  • 服务员，你好！
    Fúwùyuán, nǐ hǎo!
    Hello, waiter!
  • 请叫服务员来。
    Qǐng jiào fúwùyuán lái.
    Please call the waiter over.

服务员 is pronounced fúwùyuán and means “waiter”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
