远

Pinyin
yuǎn
English
far
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 学校离我家很远。
    Xuéxiào lí wǒ jiā hěn yuǎn.
    The school is far from my home.
  • 那儿太远了。
    Nàr tài yuǎn le.
    It is too far there.

远 is pronounced yuǎn and means “far”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
