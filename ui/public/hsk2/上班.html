上班

Pinyin
shàngbān
English
be on duty
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 爸爸早上上班。
    Bàba zǎoshang shàngbān.
    Dad goes to work in the morning.
  • 你几点上班？
    Nǐ jǐ diǎn shàngbān?
    What time do you start work?

上班 is pronounced shàngbān and means “be on duty”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
