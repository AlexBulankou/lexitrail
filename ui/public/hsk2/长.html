长

Pinyin
cháng
English
long
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 这路很长。
    Zhè lù hěn cháng.
    This road is long.
  • 他的衣服很长吗？
    Tā de yīfu hěn cháng ma?
    Are his clothes very long?

长 is pronounced cháng and means “long”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
