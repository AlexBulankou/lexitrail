过

Pinyin
guò
English
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我去过北京。
    Wǒ qù guò Běijīng.
    I have been to Beijing.
  • 您吃过那种菜吗？
    Nín chī guò nà zhǒng cài ma?
    Have you eaten that dish?
  • 我来过这里。
    Wǒ lái guò zhèlǐ.
    I have been here before.
  • 你吃过苹果吗？
    Nǐ chī guò píngguǒ ma?
    Have you eaten apples before?

过 is pronounced guò. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
