最

Pinyin
zuì
English
the most
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我最喜欢苹果。
    Wǒ zuì xǐhuān píngguǒ.
    I like apples the most.
  • 它最爱喝牛奶。
    Tā zuì ài hē niúnǎi.
    It loves drinking milk the most.
  • 我最喜欢喝茶。
    Wǒ zuì xǐhuan hē chá.
    I like drinking tea the most.
  • 她是我最好的朋友。
    Tā shì wǒ zuì hǎo de péngyǒu.
    She is my best friend.

最 is pronounced zuì and means “the most”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
