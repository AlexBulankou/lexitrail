事情

Pinyin
shìqing
English
matter
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 这件事情很不好。
    Zhè jiàn shìqing hěn bù hǎo.
    This matter is not good.
  • 我不喜欢这件事情。
    Wǒ bù xǐhuan zhè jiàn shìqing.
    I do not like this matter.

事情 is pronounced shìqing and means “matter”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
