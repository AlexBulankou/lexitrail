百

Pinyin
bǎi
English
hundred
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 它重一百公斤吗？
    Tā zhòng yī bǎi gōngjīn ma?
    Does it weigh one hundred kilograms?
  • 我有一百块吗？
    Wǒ yǒu yī bǎi kuài ma?
    Do I have one hundred yuan?
  • 我有一百块钱。
    Wǒ yǒu yì bǎi kuài qián.
    I have one hundred yuan.
  • 这个东西要一百吗？
    Zhè gè dōngxi yào yì bǎi ma?
    Does this thing cost one hundred?

百 is pronounced bǎi and means “hundred”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
