件

Pinyin
jiàn
English
piece
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我买了一件衣服。
    Wǒ mǎi le yī jiàn yīfu.
    I bought one piece of clothing.
  • 那件衣服很好。
    Nà jiàn yīfu hěn hǎo.
    That piece of clothing is good.
  • 这件东西是谁的？
    Zhè jiàn dōngxi shì shéi de?
    Whose item is this?

件 is pronounced jiàn and means “piece”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
