已经

Pinyin
yǐjīng
English
already
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我已经吃了米饭。
    Wǒ yǐjīng chī le mǐfàn.
    I have already eaten rice.
  • 它已经在这儿了。
    Tā yǐjīng zài zhèr le.
    It is already here.
  • 我已经来了。
    Wǒ yǐjīng lái le.
    I have already arrived.
  • 他们已经走了。
    Tāmen yǐjīng zǒu le.
    They have already left.

已经 is pronounced yǐjīng and means “already”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test. Recognising a word on a page and recalling it when you need it are different skills, and only the second survives a conversation — which is why LexiTrail shows you 已经 again just before you would have forgotten it, rather than on a fixed schedule.
