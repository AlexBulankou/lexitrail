等

Pinyin
děng
English
wait
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 请等一下。
    Qǐng děng yíxià.
    Please wait a moment.
  • 我在家等你。
    Wǒ zài jiā děng nǐ.
    I am waiting for you at home.

等 is pronounced děng and means “wait”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
