贵

Pinyin
guì
English
expensive
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 这手表很贵吗？
    Zhè shǒubiǎo hěn guì ma?
    Is this watch expensive?
  • 这个宾馆不贵。
    Zhège bīnguǎn bù guì.
    This hotel is not expensive.

贵 is pronounced guì and means “expensive”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
