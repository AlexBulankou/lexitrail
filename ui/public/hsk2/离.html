离

Pinyin
lí
English
indicate the distance between two objects
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我家离商店不远。
    Wǒ jiā lí shāngdiàn bù yuǎn.
    My home is not far from the store.
  • 学校离这儿很近。
    Xuéxiào lí zhèr hěn jìn.
    The school is very close to here.
  • 我家离学校很近吗？
    Wǒ jiā lí xuéxiào hěn jìn ma?
    Is my home close to the school?
  • 这里离北京远吗？
    Zhèlǐ lí Běijīng yuǎn ma?
    Is it far from here to Beijing?

离 is pronounced lí and means “indicate the distance between two objects”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
