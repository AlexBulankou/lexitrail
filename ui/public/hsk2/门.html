门

Pinyin
mén
English
door
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我开门进来。
    Wǒ kāi mén jìnlái.
    I open the door and come in.
  • 门外下雨了。
    Mén wài xiàyǔ le.
    It is raining outside the door.
  • 请开门。
    Qǐng kāi mén.
    Please open the door.
  • 门在左边吗？
    Mén zài zuǒbiān ma?
    Is the door on the left?

门 is pronounced mén and means “door”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
