红

Pinyin
hóng
English
red
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我喜欢红苹果。
    Wǒ xǐhuan hóng píngguǒ.
    I like red apples.
  • 这件衣服是红的吗？
    Zhè jiàn yīfu shì hóng de ma?
    Is this piece of clothing red?

红 is pronounced hóng and means “red”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
