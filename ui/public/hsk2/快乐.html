快乐

Pinyin
kuàilè
English
happy
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 生日快乐！
    Shēngrì kuàilè!
    Happy birthday!
  • 希望你今天快乐。
    Xīwàng nǐ jīntiān kuàilè.
    I hope you are happy today.

快乐 is pronounced kuàilè and means “happy”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
