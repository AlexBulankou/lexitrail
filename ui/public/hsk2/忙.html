忙

Pinyin
máng
English
busy
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我今天很忙。
    Wǒ jīntiān hěn máng.
    I am very busy today.
  • 他工作太忙了。
    Tā gōngzuò tài máng le.
    He is too busy at work.

忙 is pronounced máng and means “busy”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
