懂

Pinyin
dǒng
English
understand
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我听不懂汉语。
    Wǒ tīng bù dǒng Hànyǔ.
    I cannot understand Chinese by listening.
  • 他懂这个意思吗？
    Tā dǒng zhège yìsi ma?
    Does he understand this meaning?

懂 is pronounced dǒng and means “understand”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
