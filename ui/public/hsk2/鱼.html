鱼

Pinyin
yú
English
fish
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我喜欢吃鱼。
    Wǒ xǐhuān chī yú.
    I like to eat fish.
  • 那条鱼很大。
    Nà tiáo yú hěn dà.
    That fish is very big.
  • 这条鱼很好吃。
    Zhè tiáo yú hěn hǎo chī.
    This fish is delicious.

鱼 is pronounced yú and means “fish”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
