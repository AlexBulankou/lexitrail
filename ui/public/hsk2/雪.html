雪

Pinyin
xuě
English
snow
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 昨天这里下雪。
    Zuótiān zhèlǐ xià xuě.
    It snowed here yesterday.
  • 我喜欢看雪。
    Wǒ xǐhuān kàn xuě.
    I like watching the snow.
  • 今天下雪吗？
    Jīntiān xià xuě ma?
    Is it snowing today?

雪 is pronounced xuě and means “snow”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
