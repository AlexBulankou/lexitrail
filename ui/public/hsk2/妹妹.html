妹妹

Pinyin
mèimei
English
younger sister
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 妹妹在学校学习汉语。
    Mèimei zài xuéxiào xuéxí Hànyǔ.
    My younger sister studies Chinese at school.
  • 她比妹妹大三岁。
    Tā bǐ mèimei dà sān suì.
    She is three years older than her younger sister.
  • 妹妹是学生。
    Mèimei shì xuéshēng.
    My younger sister is a student.
  • 她妹妹很可爱。
    Tā mèimei hěn kě'ài.
    Her younger sister is very cute.

妹妹 is pronounced mèimei and means “younger sister”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
