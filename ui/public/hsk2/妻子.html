妻子

Pinyin
qīzi
English
wife
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 这是我的妻子。
    Zhè shì wǒ de qīzi.
    This is my wife.
  • 妻子也喜欢猫。
    Qīzi yě xǐhuān māo.
    My wife also likes cats.
  • 他妻子不在家。
    Tā qīzi bú zài jiā.
    His wife is not at home.
  • 你的妻子在工作吗？
    Nǐ de qīzi zài gōngzuò ma?
    Is your wife working?

妻子 is pronounced qīzi and means “wife”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
