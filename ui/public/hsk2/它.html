它

Pinyin
tā
English
it
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 它是我的猫。
    Tā shì wǒ de māo.
    It is my cat.
  • 您喜欢它吗？
    Nín xǐhuān tā ma?
    Do you like it?
  • 它喜欢吃米饭吗？
    Tā xǐhuan chī mǐfàn ma?
    Does it like to eat rice?

它 is pronounced tā and means “it”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
