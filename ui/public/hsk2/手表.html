手表

Pinyin
shǒubiǎo
English
watch
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 这块手表很好看。
    Zhè kuài shǒubiǎo hěn hǎokàn.
    This watch looks good.
  • 我没有手表。
    Wǒ méiyǒu shǒubiǎo.
    I do not have a watch.
  • 那块手表多少钱？
    Nà kuài shǒubiǎo duōshǎo qián?
    How much is that watch?
