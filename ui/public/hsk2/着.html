着

Pinyin
zhe
English
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 它玩着球。
    Tā wán zhe qiú.
    It is playing with a ball.
  • 我看着书，不说话。
    Wǒ kàn zhe shū, bù shuōhuà.
    I am reading a book without speaking.
  • 他看着书。
    Tā kàn zhe shū.
    He is reading the book (in a continuous state).
  • 她笑着说你好。
    Tā xiào zhe shuō nǐ hǎo.
    She smiles while saying hello to you.

着 is pronounced zhe. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
