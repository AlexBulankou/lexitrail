快

Pinyin
kuài
English
fast
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 他的飞机很快。
    Tā de fēijī hěn kuài.
    His airplane is very fast.
  • 请说快一点儿。
    Qǐng shuō kuài yìdiǎnr.
    Please speak a bit faster.

快 is pronounced kuài and means “fast”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
