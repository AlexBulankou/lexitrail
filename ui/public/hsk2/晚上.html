晚上

Pinyin
wǎnshàng
English
night
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我晚上看电影。
    Wǒ wǎnshàng kàn diànyǐng.
    I watch movies at night.
  • 您喜欢晚上学习吗？
    Nín xǐhuān wǎnshàng xuéxí ma?
    Do you like studying at night?
  • 我晚上看电视。
    Wǒ wǎnshàng kàn diànshì.
    I watch TV at night.
  • 他晚上很忙。
    Tā wǎnshàng hěn máng.
    He is busy at night.

晚上 is pronounced wǎnshàng and means “night”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
