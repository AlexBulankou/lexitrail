到

Pinyin
dào
English
arrive
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我到公司了。
    Wǒ dào gōngsī le.
    I have arrived at the company.
  • 你什么时候到家？
    Nǐ shénme shíhou dào jiā?
    When do you arrive home?

到 is pronounced dào and means “arrive”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
