休息

Pinyin
xiūxi
English
rest
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 你想休息吗？
    Nǐ xiǎng xiūxi ma?
    Do you want to rest?
  • 请在家休息一下。
    Qǐng zài jiā xiūxi yíxià.
    Please rest at home for a bit.

休息 is pronounced xiūxi and means “rest”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
