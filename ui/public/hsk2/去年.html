去年

Pinyin
qùnián
English
last year
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我去年买了这本书。
    Wǒ qùnián mǎi le zhè běn shū.
    I bought this book last year.
  • 它去年很小。
    Tā qùnián hěn xiǎo.
    It was small last year.
  • 我去年在北京。
    Wǒ qùnián zài Běijīng.
    I was in Beijing last year.
  • 去年他是学生。
    Qùnián tā shì xuéshēng.
    He was a student last year.

去年 is pronounced qùnián and means “last year”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
