旁边

Pinyin
pángbiān
English
side
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 它在我旁边坐。
    Tā zài wǒ pángbiān zuò.
    It sits beside me.
  • 教室旁边是图书馆吗？
    Jiàoshì pángbiān shì túshūguǎn ma?
    Is the library next to the classroom?
  • 我坐在你旁边。
    Wǒ zuò zài nǐ pángbiān.
    I sit next to you.
  • 教室旁边是厕所吗？
    Jiàoshì pángbiān shì cèsuǒ ma?
    Is there a restroom next to the classroom?

旁边 is pronounced pángbiān and means “side”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test. Recognising a word on a page and recalling it when you need it are different skills, and only the second survives a conversation — which is why LexiTrail shows you 旁边 again just before you would have forgotten it, rather than on a fixed schedule.
