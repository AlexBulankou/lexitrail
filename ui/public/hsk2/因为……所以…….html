因为……所以……

Pinyin
yīnwèi…suǒyǐ…
English
because…so…
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 因为我喜欢苹果，所以我买了它。
    Yīnwèi wǒ xǐhuān píngguǒ, suǒyǐ wǒ mǎi le tā.
    Because I like apples, so I bought them.
  • 因为您在这儿，所以我也来了。
    Yīnwèi nín zài zhèr, suǒyǐ wǒ yě lái le.
    Because you are here, so I came as well.
  • 因为我累，所以我不去。
    Yīnwèi wǒ lèi, suǒyǐ wǒ bù qù.
    Because I am tired, so I will not go.
  • 因为下雨，所以我在家。
    Yīnwèi xiàyǔ, suǒyǐ wǒ zài jiā.
    Because it is raining, so I am at home.

因为……所以…… is pronounced yīnwèi…suǒyǐ… and means “because…so…”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
