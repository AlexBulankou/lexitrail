日

Pinyin
rì
English
date
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 今天是五月十日。
    Jīntiān shì wǔ yuè shí rì.
    Today is May 10th.
  • 我是二月八日来的。
    Wǒ shì èr yuè bā rì lái de.
    I came on February 8th.
  • 今天是五月五日。
    Jīntiān shì wǔ yuè wǔ rì.
    Today is May 5th.
  • 他生日是哪日？
    Tā shēngrì shì nǎ rì?
    Which date is his birthday?

日 is pronounced rì and means “date”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
