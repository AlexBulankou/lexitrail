左边

Pinyin
zuǒbiān
English
left
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 它在左边。
    Tā zài zuǒbiān.
    It is on the left.
  • 我往左边看。
    Wǒ wǎng zuǒbiān kàn.
    I look to the left.
  • 猫在桌子左边。
    Māo zài zhuōzi zuǒbiān.
    The cat is on the left side of the table.
  • 我家左边是商店。
    Wǒ jiā zuǒbiān shì shāngdiàn.
    On the left side of my house is a shop.

左边 is pronounced zuǒbiān and means “left”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
