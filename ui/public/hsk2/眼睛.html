眼睛

Pinyin
yǎnjīng
English
eye
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 它的眼睛很大。
    Tā de yǎnjīng hěn dà.
    Its eyes are very big.
  • 您看见我的眼睛吗？
    Nín kànjiàn wǒ de yǎnjīng ma?
    Can you see my eyes?
  • 你眼睛很漂亮。
    Nǐ yǎnjīng hěn piàoliang.
    Your eyes are very beautiful.
  • 我想看你的眼睛。
    Wǒ xiǎng kàn nǐ de yǎnjīng.
    I want to look at your eyes.

眼睛 is pronounced yǎnjīng and means “eye”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
