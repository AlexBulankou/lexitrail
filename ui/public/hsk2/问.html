问

Pinyin
wèn
English
ask
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我能问您吗？
    Wǒ néng wèn nín ma?
    May I ask you?
  • 请问，你在吗？
    Qǐngwèn, nǐ zài ma?
    Excuse me, are you there?

问 is pronounced wèn and means “ask”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test. Recognising a word on a page and recalling it when you need it are different skills, and only the second survives a conversation — which is why LexiTrail shows you 问 again just before you would have forgotten it, rather than on a fixed schedule.
