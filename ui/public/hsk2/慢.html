慢

Pinyin
màn
English
slow
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 这出租车很慢。
    Zhè chūzūchē hěn màn.
    This taxi is very slow.
  • 请慢一点儿说话。
    Qǐng màn yìdiǎnr shuōhuà.
    Please talk more slowly.

慢 is pronounced màn and means “slow”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
