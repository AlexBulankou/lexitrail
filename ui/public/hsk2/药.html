药

Pinyin
yào
English
medicine
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 我没有带药。
    Wǒ méiyǒu dài yào.
    I did not bring any medicine.
  • 他在医院买药。
    Tā zài yīyuàn mǎi yào.
    He buys medicine at the hospital.
  • 我买了药。
    Wǒ mǎi le yào.
    I bought medicine.
  • 他有药吗？
    Tā yǒu yào ma?
    Does he have medicine?

药 is pronounced yào and means “medicine”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
