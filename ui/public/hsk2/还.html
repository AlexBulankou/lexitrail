还

Pinyin
hái
English
still
HSK level
HSK 2

Example sentences

  • 您还在看电影吗？
    Nín hái zài kàn diànyǐng ma?
    Are you still watching a movie?
  • 我还想买两本书。
    Wǒ hái xiǎng mǎi liǎng běn shū.
    I still want to buy two books.
  • 我还在学校。
    Wǒ hái zài xuéxiào.
    I am still at school.
  • 他还没来。
    Tā hái méi lái.
    He still hasn't come.

还 is pronounced hái and means “still”. It is one of the words in the HSK 2 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
