Vague in Chinese

模糊

móhu (mo2 hu5)

vague

HSK 5

Example sentences

  • 他的解释很模糊。
    Tā de jiěshì hěn móhu.
    His explanation is very vague.
  • 照片有点模糊，看不清楚。
    Zhàopiàn yǒudiǎn móhu, kàn bu qīngchu.
    The photo is a bit blurry, can't see clearly.

Other ways to say it

  • 含糊 (hánhu — vague, HSK 6)
