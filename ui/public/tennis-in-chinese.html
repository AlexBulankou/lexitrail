Tennis in Chinese

网球

wǎngqiú (wang3 qiu2)

Tennis

HSK 4

Example sentences

  • 我周末喜欢打网球。
    Wǒ zhōumò xǐhuan dǎ wǎngqiú.
    I like playing tennis on weekends.
  • 他是一位有名的网球运动员。
    Tā shì yí wèi yǒumíng de wǎngqiú yùndòngyuán.
    He is a famous tennis player.
