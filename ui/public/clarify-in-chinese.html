澄清

chéngqīng (cheng2 qing1)

clarify

Example sentences

  • 请让我澄清一下这个问题。
    Qǐng ràng wǒ chéngqīng yíxià zhège wèntí.
    Please let me clarify this issue.
  • 公司发表声明澄清误会。
    Gōngsī fābiǎo shēngmíng chéngqīng wùhuì.
    The company issued a statement to clarify the misunderstanding.
