参加

Pinyin
cānjiā
English
participate in
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 我想参加比赛。
    Wǒ xiǎng cānjiā bǐsài.
    I want to participate in the match.
  • 你参加了这个活动吗？
    Nǐ cānjiā le zhège huódòng ma?
    Did you participate in this activity?

参加 is pronounced cānjiā and means “participate in”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
