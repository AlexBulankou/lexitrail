担心

Pinyin
dānxīn
English
Worry
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 妈妈很担心你。
    Māma hěn dānxīn nǐ.
    Mom is worried about you.
  • 不要担心，我很好。
    Búyào dānxīn, wǒ hěn hǎo.
    Don't worry, I am fine.

担心 is pronounced dānxīn and means “Worry”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
