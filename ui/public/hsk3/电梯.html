电梯

Pinyin
diàntī
English
Elevator.
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 我们坐电梯上楼。
    Wǒmen zuò diàntī shàng lóu.
    We take the elevator upstairs.
  • 电梯在一层。
    Diàntī zài yī céng.
    The elevator is on the first floor.
