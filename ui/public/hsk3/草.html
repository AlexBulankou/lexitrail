草

Pinyin
cǎo
English
grass
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 小狗在草上玩。
    Xiǎo gǒu zài cǎo shàng wán.
    The puppy is playing on the grass.
  • 草是绿色的。
    Cǎo shì lǜsè de.
    The grass is green.

草 is pronounced cǎo and means “grass”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
