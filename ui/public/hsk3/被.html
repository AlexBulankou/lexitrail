被

Pinyin
bèi
English
cover
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 被子在床上。
    Bèizi zài chuáng shàng.
    The quilt is on the bed.
  • 请把被子给我。
    Qǐng bǎ bèizi gěi wǒ.
    Please give me the quilt.

被 is pronounced bèi and means “cover”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
