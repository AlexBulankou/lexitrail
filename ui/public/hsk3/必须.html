必须

Pinyin
bìxū
English
Must
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 你必须学习汉语。
    Nǐ bìxū xuéxí hànyǔ.
    You must study Chinese.
  • 我们必须早点回家。
    Wǒmen bìxū zǎodiǎn huí jiā.
    We must go home early.

必须 is pronounced bìxū and means “Must”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test. Recognising a word on a page and recalling it when you need it are different skills, and only the second survives a conversation — which is why LexiTrail shows you 必须 again just before you would have forgotten it, rather than on a fixed schedule.
