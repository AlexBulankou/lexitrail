干净

Pinyin
gānjìng
English
clean
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 房间很干净。
    Fángjiān hěn gānjìng.
    The room is very clean.
  • 请把桌子擦干净。
    Qǐng bǎ zhuōzi cā gānjìng.
    Please clean the table.

干净 is pronounced gānjìng and means “clean”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
