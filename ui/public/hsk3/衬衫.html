衬衫

Pinyin
chènshān
English
shirt
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 我有一件白衬衫。
    Wǒ yǒu yí jiàn bái chènshān.
    I have a white shirt.
  • 这件衬衫很好看。
    Zhè jiàn chènshān hěn hǎokàn.
    This shirt looks good.
