蛋糕

Pinyin
dàngāo
English
Cake
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 我喜欢吃蛋糕。
    Wǒ xǐhuan chī dàngāo.
    I like to eat cake.
  • 生日的时候吃蛋糕。
    Shēngrì de shíhou chī dàngāo.
    Eat cake on your birthday.
