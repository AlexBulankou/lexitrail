地图

Pinyin
dìtú
English
Map
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 我有一张中国地图。
    Wǒ yǒu yì zhāng Zhōngguó dìtú.
    I have a map of China.
  • 请看地图。
    Qǐng kàn dìtú.
    Please look at the map.

地图 is pronounced dìtú and means “Map”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
