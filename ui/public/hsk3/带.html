带

Pinyin
dài
English
belt
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 我带了雨伞。
    Wǒ dài le yǔsǎn.
    I brought an umbrella.
  • 请带你的书来学校。
    Qǐng dài nǐ de shū lái xuéxiào.
    Please bring your book to school.

带 is pronounced dài and means “belt”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
