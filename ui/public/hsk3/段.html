段

Pinyin
duàn
English
paragraph
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 请读这一段。
    Qǐng dú zhè yí duàn.
    Please read this paragraph.
  • 这一段很有意思。
    Zhè yí duàn hěn yǒuyìsi.
    This paragraph is interesting.

段 is pronounced duàn and means “paragraph”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test. Recognising a word on a page and recalling it when you need it are different skills, and only the second survives a conversation — which is why LexiTrail shows you 段 again just before you would have forgotten it, rather than on a fixed schedule.
