放心

Pinyin
fàngxīn
English
Don’t worry
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 你放心吧，我会做的。
    Nǐ fàngxīn ba, wǒ huì zuò de.
    Don't worry, I will do it.
  • 请放心，我很好。
    Qǐng fàngxīn, wǒ hěn hǎo.
    Please don't worry, I am fine.

放心 is pronounced fàngxīn and means “Don’t worry”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
