饿

Pinyin
è
English
hungry
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 我很饿。
    Wǒ hěn è.
    I am very hungry.
  • 你饿了吗？
    Nǐ è le ma?
    Are you hungry?

饿 is pronounced è and means “hungry”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
