船

Pinyin
chuán
English
ship
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 我们坐船去看海。
    Wǒmen zuò chuán qù kàn hǎi.
    We take a boat to see the sea.
  • 这条船很大。
    Zhè tiáo chuán hěn dà.
    This boat is very big.

船 is pronounced chuán and means “ship”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
