冰箱

Pinyin
bīngxiāng
English
Refrigerator
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 冰箱里有牛奶。
    Bīngxiāng lǐ yǒu niúnǎi.
    There is milk in the fridge.
  • 请把苹果放进冰箱。
    Qǐng bǎ píngguǒ fàng jìn bīngxiāng.
    Please put the apples in the fridge.
