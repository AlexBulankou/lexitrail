啊

Pinyin
a
English
ah
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 啊，你来了！
    A, nǐ lái le!
    Ah, you came!
  • 啊，这个菜很好吃！
    A, zhège cài hěn hǎochī!
    Ah, this dish is delicious!

啊 is pronounced a and means “ah”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
