爱好

Pinyin
àihào
English
hobby
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 我的爱好是看书。
    Wǒ de àihào shì kàn shū.
    My hobby is reading books.
  • 你有什么爱好？
    Nǐ yǒu shénme àihào?
    What hobbies do you have?

爱好 is pronounced àihào and means “hobby”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
