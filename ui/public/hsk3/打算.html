打算

Pinyin
dǎsuàn
English
Plan
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 你打算明天做什么？
    Nǐ dǎsuàn míngtiān zuò shénme?
    What do you plan to do tomorrow?
  • 我打算去北京。
    Wǒ dǎsuàn qù Běijīng.
    I plan to go to Beijing.

打算 is pronounced dǎsuàn and means “Plan”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
