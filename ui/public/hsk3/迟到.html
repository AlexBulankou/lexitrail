迟到

Pinyin
chídào
English
Late
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 上学不要迟到。
    Shàngxué búyào chídào.
    Don't be late for school.
  • 他今天迟到了。
    Tā jīntiān chídào le.
    He was late today.

迟到 is pronounced chídào and means “Late”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test. Recognising a word on a page and recalling it when you need it are different skills, and only the second survives a conversation — which is why LexiTrail shows you 迟到 again just before you would have forgotten it, rather than on a fixed schedule.
