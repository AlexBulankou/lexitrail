把

Pinyin
bǎ
English
hold
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 请把门打开。
    Qǐng bǎ mén dǎkāi.
    Please open the door.
  • 把书给我。
    Bǎ shū gěi wǒ.
    Give me the book.

把 is pronounced bǎ and means “hold”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
