方便

Pinyin
fāngbiàn
English
convenient
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 坐地铁很方便。
    Zuò dìtiě hěn fāngbiàn.
    Taking the subway is convenient.
  • 这里买东西很方便。
    Zhèlǐ mǎi dōngxi hěn fāngbiàn.
    It's convenient to buy things here.
