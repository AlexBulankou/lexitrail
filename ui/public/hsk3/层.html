层

Pinyin
céng
English
layer
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 我家在三层。
    Wǒ jiā zài sān céng.
    My home is on the third floor.
  • 教室在二层。
    Jiàoshì zài èr céng.
    The classroom is on the second floor.

层 is pronounced céng and means “layer”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
