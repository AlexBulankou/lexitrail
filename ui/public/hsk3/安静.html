安静

Pinyin
ānjìng
English
Be quiet
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 请安静一点儿。
    Qǐng ānjìng yīdiǎnr.
    Please be a little quieter.
  • 图书馆很安静。
    Túshūguǎn hěn ānjìng.
    The library is very quiet.

安静 is pronounced ānjìng and means “Be quiet”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
