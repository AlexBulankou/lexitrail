成绩

Pinyin
chéngjì
English
achievement
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 我的成绩很好。
    Wǒ de chéngjì hěn hǎo.
    My grades are good.
  • 你考试成绩怎么样？
    Nǐ kǎoshì chéngjì zěnmeyàng?
    How are your exam results?

成绩 is pronounced chéngjì and means “achievement”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
