搬

Pinyin
bān
English
move
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 我们下个月要搬家。
    Wǒmen xià gè yuè yào bānjiā.
    We will move next month.
  • 他帮我搬桌子。
    Tā bāng wǒ bān zhuōzi.
    He helps me move the table.

搬 is pronounced bān and means “move”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test.
