不但……而且……

Pinyin
búdàn…érqiě…
English
not only…but also…
HSK level
HSK 3

Example sentences

  • 他不但会说汉语，而且会说英语。
    Tā búdàn huì shuō hànyǔ, érqiě huì shuō yīngyǔ.
    He can not only speak Chinese, but also English.
  • 我不但喜欢苹果，而且喜欢香蕉。
    Wǒ búdàn xǐhuan píngguǒ, érqiě xǐhuan xiāngjiāo.
    I not only like apples, but also bananas.

不但……而且…… is pronounced búdàn…érqiě… and means “not only…but also…”. It is one of the words in the HSK 3 vocabulary, a level of the Hanyu Shuiping Kaoshi, China’s standardised Chinese proficiency test. Recognising a word on a page and recalling it when you need it are different skills, and only the second survives a conversation — which is why LexiTrail shows you 不但……而且…… again just before you would have forgotten it, rather than on a fixed schedule.
