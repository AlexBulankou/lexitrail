Reputation in Chinese

名誉

míngyù (ming2 yu4)

reputation

HSK 6

Example sentences

  • 他非常重视自己的名誉。
    Tā fēicháng zhòngshì zìjǐ de míngyù.
    He values his own reputation very much.
  • 这件事会损害公司的名誉。
    Zhè jiàn shì huì sǔnhài gōngsī de míngyù.
    This matter will damage the company's reputation.

Other ways to say it

  • 声誉 (shēngyù — reputation, HSK 6)
  • 信誉 (xìnyù — reputation, HSK 6)
